Cold Presence B
The main ambience and plays during the whole night until power runs out. This was an alternate version of "Cold Presence", which was used for the game's trailer video.

Eerie Ambience
Heard when the animatronic approached closer. There is a special area including the hallways (west and east) and the Supply Closet. Depending on if Bonnie or Chica is inside this area, the volume increases. If Foxy is past stage 2 (looking out of Pirate Cove) the volume is also increased. These are the following values: 30% when 1 threat, 50 when 2 threats, 75 when 3 threats. The volume is 100% if Freddy is inside the Office.

It is a "Eerie Ambience - Large, Scary Space, Sweeping Tones" (alternatively "SCI FI, AMBIENCE - GIANT HOLLOW TUBE SECTOR") sample from SoundDogs.

Surreal Ominous Ambience
The power-out ambience. Note that the ambiance is also used in Ultimate Custom Night.

It is a "Surreal Ominous Background - Dark, Deep, Wind Elements, Haunted House Ambience, Flowing" sample from Sound Ideas.
